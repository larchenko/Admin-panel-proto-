The screens themselves — the last layer of Atomic Design, where a template is filled with the real thing. Everything here is assembled from the design system next door and adds no drawing of its own; if a screen needs something the system has not got, the component comes first.

The Admin panel has six areas, in the order the product owner gave them. One is built. The other five are listed because a design system that shows only what exists says nothing about what is still owed.
