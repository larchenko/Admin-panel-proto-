What to notice

  • One column. Eyes travel down, not across.
  • ISIN and Currency share a row because both are short and belong together.
  • The invalid field shows the error in place of its description.
  • Actions sit in a footer, primary on the right, and the form scrolls as one unit.
  • Inputs, radios and checkboxes are native elements; the select is custom but follows the same keyboard rules: Tab, Space, arrows, Enter, Escape.
