Checkbox

Spartan: hlm-checkbox · shadcn: checkbox.tsx

Independent yes/no choices, or several from a list. Wrap control and text in a label.choice so the whole row is clickable.

Read-only, in a table

A boolean a row reports but nobody edits there — Pricer on the instruments list. It is a span, not an input: no tab stop, no cursor, nothing announced as a control. The value reaches a screen reader through the sr-only label beside it.

Why not a tick and a dash
Both are thin marks of about the same size, so a column of them reads as texture rather than as two states. A filled square against an empty square differs in shape, weight and colour at once — and still differs with the colour taken away.
Why not a real disabled checkbox
Disabled means "you could change this, but not now". Nothing here is ever editable in the list, and the 50% opacity would undo the contrast we just bought.
The one difference from the control
No shadow-xs. Same size, radius, border and fill; the missing lift is the cue that it does nothing.
For the developer
Not a Spartan component — plain markup. Do not reach for hlm-checkbox with [disabled] here.
